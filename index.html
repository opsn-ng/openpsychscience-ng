Open Psychological Science Nigeria (OPSN).

OPSN is a community of individuals within (and outside) the fields of psychological, cognitive, social, health, and medical sciences in Nigeria interested in learning how to conduct and produce trustworthy and rigorous research through adopting and advancing open science practices.

Join the community What we do

What we do

Journal Club

We read and discuss the articles that either shaped the open science movement or are relevant to making better science. Sessions are open to anyone.

Workshops and training

Practical sessions on the tools of open research: computational methods, version control, reproducible analysis, and responsible data sharing.

Resources

Guides, templates and reading lists.

Community

A place for students and early-career researchers to find collaborators, ask questions, and get feedback on work in progress.
